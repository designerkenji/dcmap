# The two dropped points: Crusoe/Google “Goodnight Campus”, Claude TX

Identified 2026-09-06, confidence: High. Independent lines of evidence agree: (1) reverse geocoding puts both pins in Armstrong County; (2) OSM places Lima Road at latitude 35.0168 — between the two pins — and a brand-new 345 kV substation complex (ways 1265934372 / 1525002844) within 50-110 m of the SW pin, on Sharyland's 345 kV line; (3) TDLR TABS filings put two 484,954 sq ft data centers owned by GN DC1 LLC at 10001 Lima Rd, Claude TX; (4) local press locates the Crusoe site "off Lima Road, just east of Highway 207," south of Claude; (5) statewide/national press ties the campus to Google, the Goodnight wind farm co-location case, and the 933 MW gas permit. No competing candidate exists at these coordinates..

**Identity:** Crusoe "Goodnight Campus" AI data center (permit name "Project Llano" / "Llano Building 1 & 2"), 10001 Lima Rd, Claude, TX 79019, Armstrong County — announced in November 2025 as a Google data center site. The two pins are one campus: the NE pin (35.019478, -101.305776) falls on the data-center building pads off Lima Road, and the SW pin (35.014152, -101.311503) falls directly on the site's new fenced 345 kV generation + transmission substation pair tapping Sharyland Utilities' 345 kV CREZ line.

**Operator chain:** Developer/builder: Crusoe Energy Systems LLC, through owner SPV GN DC1 LLC ("GN" = Goodnight). Tenant/anchor: Google (part of its announced $40B Texas investment; Google also agreed to acquire the campus's permitted 933 MW gas plant). Related parties: Ensign Infrastructure (SPV holding the wind-powered load for the PUCT co-location case) and Serena Energy's Goodnight 1 Wind Farm (265.5 MW, GOODNIT1), whose ERCOT point of interconnection the load co-locates behind.

**Kind and scale:** Hyperscale AI-compute data-center campus (not crypto). Full build: ~7 buildings, ~3.4M sq ft, up to ~1,030 MW facility load; later scaled to 4-6 buildings. Buildings 1 & 2 are 484,954 sq ft each at ~$292M apiece (TDLR filings, registered 2025-08-21, completion ~Aug 2026 — matching what was visible when the pins were dropped 2026-08-30); later buildings 805,380 sq ft. Power: ERCOT grid via the Sharyland 345 kV CREZ line, net-metered against the co-located 265.5 MW Goodnight wind farm (ERCOT required 525.5 MW of the load be curtailable on 30 min notice), plus a TCEQ-permitted 933 MW on-site gas plant (GE Vernova / Solar Turbines). Closed-loop cooling; ~80-100 permanent jobs; county tax abatement amended 2025-10-17 to $1.35M/yr per operational building.

**Detail:**

Geometry: the pins straddle Lima Road (OSM lat 35.0168) about 10-11 km SSE of Claude, TX and ~5-7 km east of SH 207, matching every published locator for the site ("off Lima Road, just east of Highway 207", "10001 Lima Rd, Claude TX 79019"). The SW pin (35.014152, -101.311503) is a direct hit on the campus's new 345 kV substation complex — a fenced transmission substation ~50 m away and a fenced generation substation ~110 m away, both tied into Sharyland Utilities' N-S 345 kV double-circuit CREZ line that passes through the site at longitude ~-101.310. The NE pin (35.019478, -101.305776), ~700 m away, sits on the building-pad area where Llano Buildings 1-4 were under construction. Timeline fits the 2026-08-30 pin drop: TDLR registered Buildings 1 & 2 on 2025-08-21 with completion targeted for ~Aug 2026, so both the substation and at least the first hyperscale halls would have been prominent on fresh satellite imagery when the points were hand-dropped. Naming note: 'Goodnight' refers to the nearby hamlet and the co-located Goodnight 1 Wind Farm (Serena Energy, COD Dec 2023, GOODNIT1), not to the campus being at Goodnight itself — the wind turbines are ~15-20 km further east, while the shared ERCOT interconnection and the data center are here on Lima Road. Alternative candidates ruled out: Fermi America's 'Project Matador'/Advanced Energy & Intelligence Campus is NE of Amarillo near Pantex in Carson County (~35.3N, -101.5W), the wrong side of Amarillo; no crypto-mining or standalone grid-scale project is recorded at these coordinates. Recommended labels for the registry: treat both dots as one asset — 'Crusoe Goodnight Campus (Google) — Project Llano', operator Crusoe Energy Systems / GN DC1 LLC, tenant Google, type AI data center, with the SW dot optionally sub-labeled 'Goodnight 345 kV substation / GOODNIT1 POI'.
